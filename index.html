ALGERIA AI - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Introduction

ALGERIA AI is a Discord bot designed to provide AI assistance and community management features for the ALGERIA CITY ROLEPLAY community.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, the bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, messages submitted to the bot, commands, support tickets, and staff activity records.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate the bot, respond to commands, provide AI assistance, manage support tickets, and support server administration.

4. AI Services

Content submitted to AI features may be sent to the configured AI provider to generate responses.

5. Data Storage and Sharing

Information may be stored when needed to operate the bot and may be processed by service providers. We do not sell personal information.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained as reasonably necessary. Users may contact the bot administrators to request deletion of eligible information.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information, although absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the ALGERIA CITY ROLEPLAY server administrators through the official Discord server.
